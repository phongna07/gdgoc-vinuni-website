📌 On Saturday, November 11th, 2023, we kicked off the Workshop Challenge Series with an incredible Session 1! Led by Linh Truong, the Tech Lead of GDSC VinUni, this session aimed to introduce our members to the upcoming Solution Challenge competition by GDSC Global, scheduled for January 2024. 💡💪

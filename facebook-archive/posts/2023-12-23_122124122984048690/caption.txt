🎉 Trong buổi Lễ phát động cuộc thi Google Developer Student Clubs - Hackathon Vietnam 2024 (GDSC Hackathon Vietnam 2024) diễn ra tại Đại học Bách Khoa Hà Nội vào ngày 16/12/2023, Ban tổ chức rất vinh hạnḥ khi nhận được sự quan tâm và ủng hộ từ các vị đại biểu khách quý đến từ các đơn vị bảo trợ chuyên môn, các quý thầy cô và các bạn học sinh sinh viên. 

🌍 Chủ đề năm nay hướng tới cách giải quyết các thách thức trong 17 Mục tiêu Phát triển Bền vững của Liên Hợp Quốc (SDGs). Các thông tin cụ thể về thể lệ, cơ cấu giải thưởng và giải đáp thắc mắc đã được ban tổ chức trình bày trong lễ khai mạc. 

✨ GDSC VinUniversity rất vinh dự được góp mặt tại buổi lễ cùng Thầy Phạm Huy Hiệu - Giám đốc Entrepreneurship at VinUniversity, đồng thời cũng là phó giám đốc VISHC, và chị Alex Phương - Giám đốc Entrepreneurship at VinUniversity.  

👏 Cuộc thi GDSC Hackathon Vietnam 2024 chính thức khởi động. Các bạn hãy theo dõi để kịp thời nhận thông tin từ Google Developer Student Clubs - Hackathon Vietnam và GDSC VinUni nhé!

🎉 During the launching ceremony of the Google Developer Student Clubs - Hackathon Vietnam 2024 (GDSC Hackathon Vietnam 2024) contest taking place at Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST) on December 16, 2023, the Organizing Committee was very honored to receive the participation and support from distinguished guests from professional sponsoring units, teachers and students.  

🌍 This year's theme focuses on how to solve challenges in the United Nations' 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). Specific information about the rules, award structure, and answers to questions was presented by the Organizing Committee during the ceremony.  

✨ GDSC VinUniversity was very honored to be present there with Mr. Pham Huy Hieu - Director of Entrepreneurship at VinUniversity, Vice Director of VISHC, and Ms. Alex Phuong - Director of Entrepreneurship at VinUniversity.  

👏 GDSC Hackathon Vietnam 2024 officially kicks off. Please follow along to promptly receive information from Google Developer Student Clubs - Hackathon Vietnam and GDSC VinUni!

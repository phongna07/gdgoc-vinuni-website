[Sonder 2025](/events/sonder-2025) connected GDG on Campus chapters in Vietnam and the Philippines for an evening of talks. Here is what stayed with us.

## Cleo Credo: from individual contributor to tech leader

> "We are engineers, we are creatives, we are there to solve problems."

Cleo is an experienced software engineer with a love for web technology. Her talk, *Navigating Your Career in Tech*, gave a practical view of one of the most important transitions in the industry: growing from someone who contributes alone into someone who leads. Her real stories and her advice on self-reflection helped attendees think more clearly about where they are heading.

![Cleo Credo's talk](/images/events/sonder-2025/06.jpg)

## Ria Cordero: designing conversion-focused websites

> "Building a website is more than just bits of pixels and lines of codes."

## Eldrych Ken Espiritu: Google Sheets with AI

> "When data meets AI, it's where the magic starts. Save time, use AI."

## Damian Sire: sustainable web and AI with TensorFlow

The fourth talk closed the evening with a look at building for the web and with AI in a way that lasts.

Thank you to every speaker, and to the chapters in both countries that made the event happen.

> **Q:** Hi admin, I can't find Google Developer Student Club – GDSC VinUni on Facebook any more. What do I do?
>
> **A:** Hello, and thanks for getting in touch. We are coming back for a new term with a new name.

That is how we announced it in September 2024. After a year as **Google Developer Student Club VinUniversity**, the club became:

## Google Developer Group on Campus: VinUni

The change was part of a global move in which student clubs joined the wider Google Developer Groups family.

![The rename announcement](/images/articles/gdsc-to-gdg-on-campus/02.jpg)

## A month of keeping a secret

For a month after the rename, our page name, avatar and cover photo still said GDSC VinUni. People noticed. We were keeping things under wraps for a reason.

On 24 November 2024 we unveiled the new visual identity, and with it a new stretch of the journey.

Bring your laptop. It is no longer GDSC VinUni. It is GDG on Campus: VinUni.

Team **VinWin** was Hoàng Anh Minh, a member of GDSC VinUni's Tech Web team, and his teammate Nguyễn Đình Phong. In November 2023 they entered **AI Challenge: ChatGPT Problem Solvers**, hosted by GDSC HUST, against teams from HUST, BKU, HCMUS, UIT and others.

They did it in the middle of their first midterm season at VinUni.

## What they brought home

- Runner-up overall
- Highest rate of correct code in the final
- Top 3 coder in the group stage (Hoàng Anh Minh)
- Best scientific report (Nguyễn Đình Phong)
- An award for presentation

## In Phong's words

> Proud? Yes! We went in as a team of two while other teams had three to five people, and we gave the competition two full days with a Calculus midterm waiting on Monday. That is already something to be proud of. Wearing a VinUni shirt into a hall full of red shirts took nerve. Walking out with respect and interest from sponsors, lecturers and fellow contestants at HUST was a success. And above all, giving everything alongside 400 contestants from across the country was an experience to remember.

Congratulations, VinWin.

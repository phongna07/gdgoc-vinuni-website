The interview is how we find people who fit the club. It is just as much our chance to understand you: what you care about and what you want to grow into.

## Tech

**What we look for.** Skills matter, and so do attitude and willingness to learn. You should know the basics of at least one language, Python or JavaScript/TypeScript. Experience in web and backend, AI and data, or cloud and dev tools is a plus, but you do not need all of it.

- **Review your CV.** Be ready to go deeper on your projects, your role, the difficulties and what you learned.
- **Know your club.** Learn about GDGoC VinUni, why you want to join, and how much time you can give.
- **Show your learning attitude.** A small self-made project, or the way you dig into a problem, counts.
- **Think out loud.** For scenario questions we care about your reasoning more than a perfect answer.

## Operations & HR

**What we look for.** Responsibility, attention to detail, the ability to organise and plan, and flexibility when things change. Being proactive, on time and easy to work with matters a lot to us.

- **Understand the role.** Read the job description and think about how you would contribute.
- **Think ahead.** Picture the tasks behind a successful event and how you would get them done with others.
- **Show your initiative.** Little experience is not a barrier. Care, initiative and openness all count.
- **Share your experience.** If you have organised events, be specific about your role and what you learned.
- **Stay calm and logical.** There is no single right answer to a scenario question.

## Marketing & Communications

**What we look for.** Creative thinking, the ability to carry a message, and a sharp eye for the audience. Beyond writing and design, we value experimentation, adaptability and teamwork.

- **Think creatively.** Share how you develop an idea and why you made your choices.
- **Know your message.** A beautiful piece that says the wrong thing does not work.
- **Understand your audience.** Put yourself in the reader's place.
- **Show your potential.** No professional portfolio needed. Posts, designs, videos and personal projects all show what you can do.
- **Stay curious.** Follow trends, try things, and be open to feedback.

## A little reminder

Do your homework, be honest, ask away, and be yourself.

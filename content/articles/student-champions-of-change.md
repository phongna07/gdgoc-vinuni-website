In May 2024, VinUniversity honoured GDSC VinUniversity with the **Student Champions of Change** award.

The award recognises individuals or groups at VinUniversity who have introduced groundbreaking initiatives, achieved outstanding results, and contributed significantly to the community, encouraging innovation, a pioneering spirit and social responsibility.

## What it means to us

It is a recognition of what the club did in its first year, and a strong reason to keep going. We are committed to raising the quality of our projects and events, and to giving more back to the VinUniversity community and to technology.

![With the award](/images/articles/student-champions-of-change/02.jpg)

To the collaborators and friends of the club: thank you. We hope to keep earning your support.

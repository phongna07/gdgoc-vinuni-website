GDGoC VinUni began with one student who wanted a community for people who love technology. That student was **Lưu Bảo Yến**, a Business Analytics major with a minor in AI.

## Starting from zero

Yến founded the chapter in September 2023, when it was still called GDSC VinUni. According to the club's own record, the chapter went on to be named Most Impactful Chapter in Vietnam, and she was recognised as Global GDSC Lead of the Year 2023.

She did not stop at one campus. She initiated Hackathon Vietnam 2024, connecting more than five chapters across the country into one competition for students, and she led that project remotely while on exchange in Hong Kong.

## In her own words

In mid-2024 the club interviewed her as the Gen 1 lead stepped down.

**Why Business Analytics rather than a STEM major?**

> When I was choosing a major, very few girls around me picked STEM. I was good at the natural sciences, but for some reason I did not choose to pursue them at university.

**What was hardest about adding an AI minor?**

> It was not really that hard. At VinUniversity I could take courses from other majors, so I could join maths and programming classes and pursue the minor.

**Which project did you enjoy most as lead?**

> Organising GDSC Hackathon Vietnam 2024. The club was in its first year and was chosen as a host venue. Keeping a project of that size going while studying abroad was unforgettable. It was hard, but I believe the organising team and I did it well.

**And your greatest joy in it?**

> Looking at the list of teams and the audience, I saw that the ratio of men to women had become fairly balanced. I hope that diversity is kept and grows in the hackathons to come.

## What she does now

Yến now works in research at the Center for Environmental Intelligence and the College of Business and Management at VinUniversity, applying AI and technology to environmental problems.

## Her advice

You do not need to know everything, and you do not need to wait until you are ready. Sometimes it is enough to be curious enough to try, brave enough to step outside what is comfortable, and stubborn enough to keep going.

That is what **Build Your Next** means to us. Your next chapter starts with what you choose to build today.

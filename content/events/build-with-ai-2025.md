This session was reserved for the 36 teams that made it through the preliminary round of GDGoC Hackathon Vietnam 2025 into the Online Hackathon round.

## On the agenda

- **Gemini 2.0 Flash.** What the model can do and how developer communities use it, followed by a lab calling the Gemini API from Python.
- **NotebookLM.** Using it as a research assistant to sharpen an idea and move a product forward.
- **APAC Solution Challenge.** The latest information on the regional competition.

The goal was simple: give every team tools they could put into their project the same week.

DevFest is the biggest yearly gathering of the Google developer community in Hanoi. In 2023 it reached its tenth edition, and GDSC VinUni, only weeks old, took part as a community partner with a booth of its own.

## What was on

- Tech talks across five topics: Android, Firebase, Machine Learning, Cloud and Flutter
- Eight speakers, including Google Developer Experts from Vietnam and abroad
- The MIRO Game Contest hackathon
- More than 500 expected attendees and over 20 partner communities

For a brand-new club, the booth was our first chance to meet the wider GDG community in Hanoi face to face and to introduce VinUniversity's chapter to it.

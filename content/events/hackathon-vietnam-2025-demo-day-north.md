On 19 April 2025 the best teams from northern Vietnam came to VinUniversity to pitch at the regional Demo Day of GDGoC Hackathon Vietnam 2025. The year's theme was **Responsible AI**.

## Through to the national final

- **DataFlow** (FPT University Hòa Lạc): a tutoring platform that uses AI to match teachers with students based on what students post.
- **Cộng đồng Chat GPT** (Hanoi University of Science and Technology): a learning platform that assesses a student's level and personalises their study path.

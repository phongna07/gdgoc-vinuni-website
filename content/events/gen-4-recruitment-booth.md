During Generation 4 recruitment we set up a booth in the main hall of Building I so that anyone curious could talk to members in person before applying.

Visitors could ask about the club and the four stations, and try **Bug Squasher**, a mini-game made for the occasion.

Whether you care about technology, communications, or just want a community to learn with, the message was the same: build your next, and build it with us.

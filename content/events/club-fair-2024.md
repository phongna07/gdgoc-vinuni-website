Every academic year at VinUniversity starts with the Club Fair. In 2024 we were there to welcome new students, introduce the club on stage, and talk to anyone curious about the Google developer world.

The fair ran alongside recruitment for our second generation, with positions open in:

- **Tech:** AI, Data Science
- **Non-Tech:** Media, Operations & HR, External Relations

A few days later the club announced [its new name](/articles/gdsc-to-gdg-on-campus).

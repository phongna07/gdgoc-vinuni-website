**Sonder: Designing a Future that Lasts** brought together developers, innovators and sustainability advocates for one evening online. It was organised jointly by GDG on Campus chapters in Vietnam and the Philippines.

## Who organised it

Ten chapters from Vietnam took part, including VinUniversity, Hanoi University of Science and Technology, PTIT, UIT, HCMIU, FPT University HCMC, HCMC University of Transport, HCMUS, Duy Tân University and HCMUT. They were joined by nineteen chapters from the Philippines.

## What happened

- Keynotes from engineers and designers working in the industry
- Discussion with the speakers
- Cross-border networking between student developers

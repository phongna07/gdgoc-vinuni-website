CECS Day is the largest yearly event of VinUniversity's College of Engineering and Computer Science. On 8 June 2024, GDSC VinUni ran a booth with mini-games and prizes.

As the student developer club focused on Google technologies, we were glad to be part of the day, and we look forward to more work with the College on activities that matter to students.

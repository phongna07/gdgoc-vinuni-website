GDSC Hackathon Vietnam is the yearly competition run by Google developer student chapters across the country. The 2024 season opened with a launch ceremony at Hanoi University of Science and Technology on 16 December 2023.

## The theme

Teams were asked to tackle challenges from the United Nations' 17 Sustainable Development Goals. The organisers presented the rules and prize structure and answered questions from students, lecturers and partner organisations.

## VinUni at the launch

GDSC VinUniversity attended alongside representatives of Entrepreneurship at VinUniversity. Registration ran from 21 December 2023 to 10 January 2024, with an online preliminary round.

This season mattered a great deal to us: in its very first year, the chapter was chosen as a host venue for the hackathon. Read more in [our founder's story](/articles/founder-story).

After three rounds and nearly 100 projects, GDGoC Hackathon Vietnam 2025 closed on 17 May 2025 at Hanoi University of Science and Technology. Hundreds of students from universities across the country came to watch the top five teams.

Under the theme **Responsible AI**, teams presented AI solutions to real problems in the environment, education and society.

## Results

| Award | Team |
| --- | --- |
| Champion | Data Flow |
| Runner-up | LETME |
| Third place | 404 Brain Not Found, HackOff, Cộng đồng Chat GPT |
| Most loved project | HackOff |
| Most promising project | Data Flow |
| Talented individual | Nguyễn Đình Phong (LETME), Phạm Chí Bằng (Cộng đồng Chat GPT) |

## The programme

The morning opened with a talk by Nguyễn Hòa Bình, Chairman of NextTech Group, followed by pitches from the five finalists and a debate round between the two strongest teams.

"Recommendation System: The Technology Behind Engaging Suggestions" took place on 17 January 2025 with students from across VinUni.

## The agenda

1. **A history of recommender systems.** Phạm Đình Khánh traced the algorithms from early static rule-based methods to modern deep learning, and showed how each step made recommendations more personal.
2. **Deep learning recommenders.** A closer look at the main deep learning approaches, their strengths and weaknesses, and ways around their limits.
3. **Hands-on lab.** Participants built a collaborative filtering system with deep neural networks, guided by the speaker.

Thank you to everyone who joined and to Mr Khánh for a session that was as practical as it was clear.

The Global EntrePrep Incubator is run by VinUniversity's Entrepreneurship Lab together with Next Challenge Asia Foundation and Reactor School. It gives young founders and early startups a structured place to build, and it ends with a public Startup Showcase.

GDSC VinUni joined the 2023 showcase as a partner, helping the programme reach student builders at VinUniversity and beyond.

We are grateful to the Entrepreneurship Lab for inviting us, and to our friends at GDSC ACT and GDSC Phenikaa, who helped us deliver our part of the event.

On Sunday 2 June 2024 we held a small sharing session with friends from other GDSC chapters in Hanoi.

**Bảo Yến**, lead of GDSC VinUni, talked about her exchange semester at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology: the courses, the extracurricular scene, student life, and what day-to-day living in Hong Kong is like.

HKUST is known for its strength in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence and in Engineering & Technology, so there was plenty to ask about. We hope to run more evenings like this one.

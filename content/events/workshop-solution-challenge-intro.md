The Workshop Challenge Series was our first run of internal training, built to get Generation 1 ready for competition season.

Session 1 was led by **Trương Khánh Linh**, Tech Lead of GDSC VinUni. It introduced the Solution Challenge, the global GDSC competition scheduled for January 2024.

## The brief

- Teams of four
- Pick one or more of the United Nations' 17 Sustainable Development Goals
- Build a working solution using Google technologies

The session closed with a group photo, and Session 2 followed the next weekend.
